How to read this

  • Crowding is how full the bus usually is on that stretch at that time, on October 2026 weekdays or weekends.
  • On board is a range, because e-tickets are only ~3–4% of all tickets and are scaled up.
  • Confidence says how sure we are of the crowding level shown: high when many tickets back it, or when the level would not change even with sampling noise. Low mostly at night.
  • Walks of up to 400 m are offered at the start, at a change and at the end, when they save time.
